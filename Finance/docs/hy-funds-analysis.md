## How to read this

- **The decision rule** (skill, Standing parameters) judges yield against risk:
  - **E** = Base excess return over 3M NIBOR, window average, after fees and credit loss.
  - **R** = stress loss = Base 2027 return − Bear 2027 return, in pp.
  - **Market reference** = the Nordic HY index's E / R after a typical 0.6% fee: (2.75 − 0.6) /
    8.47 = **0.254** (0.216 with Bear read as a shock size; 0.196 until 4 Nov).
  - **Attractive:** E / R ≥ 0.254 and E ≥ 1.0 pp. **Marginal:** E / R 0.191–0.254 (75–100%), or
    E 0.5–1.0 pp. **Unattractive:** below that. "On the line" marks a fund within ~0.02 of the
    reference.
  - **Step-up over the reference fund** = (E_fund − E_Arctic) ÷ (R_fund − R_Arctic): the extra
    excess return per unit of extra stress loss for moving from Arctic Return into the fund. The
    market's step-up is (2.15 − 0.15) ÷ (8.47 − 1.41) = **0.283**.
- **The price on screen:** NAV per unit in NOK (Nordnet, 6 Nov) is shown next to each verdict. A
  fund has no hurdle price: its verdict rests on E / R.
- **October is in the stub.** Each fund's own October return is in the stub (the HY baseline's
  instruction), and its carry starts at its 30 Oct yield. Marks made in October are therefore in
  E once, and the higher yield they bring is counted from November.
- **Rounding:** ratios are shown to two decimals in the tables. Sensitivities use three.
- **Tags:** `[F]` fact · `[E]` external or secondary · `[A]` own assumption · `[?]` unknown.
