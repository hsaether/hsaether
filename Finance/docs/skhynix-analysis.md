## Summary

**The memory cycle** (read from `docs/ai-market-bbb.md`, H2 Memory cycle and its 18 Nov turn call; no memory forecast is made here)
- **Where:** late upcycle. Contract prices still rise (Q4-26 conventional DRAM +10–15%; Q1-27 +3–8%), but momentum has slowed (PC DRAM "slower than last quarter", DDR5 spot soft); margins are records (SK hynix 80% operating in Q3); the shares peaked in June (−64% from 2,987,000 at the 23 Nov close).
- **What turns it:** new supply from 2H-27 into 2028 (SK hynix Yongin fab 1 in 2027, M17 Dec 2028, Y2 Jun 2029; Samsung's 2026 capex +60% and higher in 2027; Micron 1δ / G11 in 2H-27; CXMT) meeting AI capex digestion after the contracted 2027 build (K1 Base 1,120 → 1,170 → 1,170bn). LTAs soften the fall for the leaders and cap the rise.
- **The AI BBB's call (18 Nov, 60%, moved Base):** 2027 kept at **0.90**; **2028–30 deeper: .50 / .32 / .28** of CY27 consensus (was .60 / .40 / .35); revenue index 138 / 108 / 95. Families: slowing contract-price momentum (L2), the supplier capex step-up (L4), memory shares −33% to −41% (L5); against: HBM4 sold out, LTAs (L1). Reversal signpost: Q1-27 DRAM contract ≥ +10% q/q with spot above contract (early Jan).
- **What the price implies:** at KRW 1,085,000 this build earns k if 2028–30 earnings come in at **0.47× the new Base** (.24 / .15 / .13 of CY27 consensus) and 0% at 0.29×. A 2019-depth fall (.45 / .30 / .25) still returns ~33%. **The price discounts a fall about twice as deep as the AI BBB's already-deeper Base.** The AI BBB layer method gave 0.72× (18 Nov, at the 18 Nov price); this build is lower because net cash is ~16% of the price and the cash return is fast.

- **Bear:** the decision Bear (exit P/E 8 at the trough) returns ~23% and clears k, because the price is ~1.7× core earnings and re-rates up. **Capped at today's core multiple (1.67×): −4% a year, −31% in 2027, −8% of capital** — the realistic share-price stress. ADR in the capped Bear (premium gone): −13% a year, −29% of capital.
- **Price versus our call (Step 6b): with the market on the direction, the price deeper than our Base.** The market discounts a 2028–30 fall ~53% below the AI BBB's deepened Base; the 20–23 Nov fall came on the NVIDIA accounting-probe report and the US chip sell-off, not on a memory print. The named reason it may be wrong: SK hynix's LTAs on > 50% of 2027–28 capacity and its net cash (16% of the price) carry it through a trough the price treats as permanent.

**Base in brief**
