## Summary

**Verdict**
- **The company still clears k, with a smaller margin than in version 1.** On the Seoul common
  share (KRW 232,000, +7.7% since 23 Oct, −4.1% on the report day) the Base IRR is **23.3% a year**
  (KRW; NOK the same in Base) against k = 12%. Hurdle price **P_k ≈ KRW 306,200: margin of safety
  +32.0%.**
  - **Most of the fall in the margin is the price.** The price step takes the margin from +43.3% to
    +33.3%; the SK hynix template's two method fixes add 2 points back; **the report itself moves
    the hurdle by −2.5%** (P_k 313,958 → 306,225). Operating profit came in as guided (KRW 107.4tn),
    but the won's rise cost a −9.5tn financial result, 2026 capex is 84.4tn (version 1: ~70tn) and
    rising in 2027, and the flat-price Q4 estimate sits below the consensus version 1 used.
  - Samsung trades at **3.21× calendar-2027 consensus EPS** (KRW 72,281; 33 analysts; not yet
    revised for Q3) and **2.90× after net cash**. SK hynix trades at 3.25× (KRW 1,522,000), Micron
    at ~5.0×.
  - The AI baseline's memory path takes memory earnings to **0.90 of consensus in 2027, then 0.60,
    0.40 and 0.35 in 2030**. With the non-memory businesses recovering as memory costs fall, Base
    **2030 core EPS is KRW 26,963** (~4× the 2025 EPS of 6,605). On that fall the price is **7.8×
    Base 2030 core earnings after net cash** (version 1: 7.1×; SK hynix 8.1×), against an exit P/E
    of 10.
  - **Net cash is ~10% of the price** (KRW ~22,400 a share at the valuation date, after the 30tn Q3
    dividend that today's buyer no longer gets, the tax owed on 2026 profits and the 2026 bonus
    owed to staff; ~29,400 at year-end).
  - **The January 2027 decision comes to the buyer:** the rest of the 2026 return (KRW 55–75tn; 65
    assumed = KRW 9,895 a share, ~4.3% of the price), with "a substantial share repurchase and
    cancellation program" and a possible tilt of buybacks to the preference shares.
  - The margin rests more on the AI-era mid-cycle than before: on its own mid-cycle (2030 earnings
    from 2027) the Base gives **16.8%** (version 1: 20.1%); at today's core multiple held to exit,
    16.5%; at 2030 memory earnings ×0.6, 13.0% (still a pass); at ×0.5, 10.0% (**fails**).
- **Bear no longer re-rates the share up by much:**
  - On the decision Bear (layer exit P/E 8 at the trough) the share returns **5.3%**, capital
    preserved (+15.9%). The 2027 Bear return is +1.9% (version 1: +8.9%).
  - **Capped at today's multiple** (diagnostic, AI BBB method): **−10.5% a year**, −45% in 2027,
    capital −26%.
  - The catalyst is the AI BBB financing break and memory overshoot inside the equity recession.
    The balance sheet is never at risk: net cash grows every year, FCF positive in every scenario.
- **Bull: 34.3%** (P_k KRW 393,300). The price is below the Base hurdle, so none of the Bull is
  priced.
- **What the price needs for 12%** (any one): an exit P/E of **6.0** on 2030 core EPS (Base 10); or
  2030 memory earnings at **0.20 of CY27 consensus** (core EPS KRW 16,325, ~2.5× 2025; Base 0.35);
  or the whole memory path **27% lower**.
- **The preference share is still the better line for the same company.** 005935 (KRW 183,900)
  carries the same EPS and dividend per share at a **20.7% discount**: Base **25.6%**, P_k KRW
  253,600, margin of safety **+37.9%** if the discount holds; Bear 3.4% if it widens to 30%. Samsung
  now says it will "actively" consider raising the preference share of its buybacks (Bull: the
  discount narrows to 15%).
  - The **London GDRs** (SMSN = 25 common, SMSD = 25 preference) trade at parity with Seoul
    (+0.4% / +0.5% on 28 Oct). SMSN's last close ($4,524, before the report) is 4% above parity with
    today's Seoul close: Base 21.6%, hurdle **$5,729 per GDR**. The GDRs were **not found on
    Nordnet** (`docs/kospi-analysis.md`, 29 Oct); the reachable route is IQQK.
- **Against World, its layer and HY:** Base 23.3% against World 5.2%, the HY market 6.8%, the H2
  basket 12.5% (NOK, 23 Oct prices) and the layer method's 16.6% (at KRW 215,500). **E / R = 0.249

- **In line with the layer at the start:** Q3 operating profit as guided and at consensus; net

## Verdict and caveat

- **Verdict (Seoul common): clears.** Base margin of safety **+32.0%** (Base IRR 23.3% vs k 12%). It
  survives the decision Bear (5.3%, capital +15.9%), but **not the capped Bear** (−10.5%, −26% of
  capital, −45% in 2027). The balance sheet survives every scenario.
- **Verdict (preference): clears, with more margin.** Base 25.6%, margin of safety **+37.9%** at KRW
  183,900 with the discount held; the same EPS and dividends at a 21% lower price, and a company
  that says it may tilt buybacks toward it.
- **What the price needs:** nothing in Base: the price is 24% below the Base hurdle (P_k 32% above
  P).
- **What would flip it:**
  - the H2 mid-cycle being far lower than the AI BBB's (2030 memory earnings ×0.5 already fails:
    memory's pre-AI history);
  - DRAM and HBM prices falling q/q in 2027 (financing break, P4/P5, SK hynix's Yongin and CXMT
    capacity, the industry's 2027 capex step-up arriving early);
  - a 2027–29 return policy well below 50% of FCF *and* a market that values the cash pile at a
    deep discount (payout 0% with cash at 50%: Base 14.6%, still a pass; it lowers the margin, it
    does not flip it on its own);
  - CY27 consensus cut hard after Q3 (the won; memory −5%: 21.4%).
- **Caveat:**
  - This is a margin-of-safety screen, not an intrinsic valuation, a price target or personal
    advice.
  - The memory path is the AI BBB's. The non-memory paths, the capex path, the payout after 2026,
    the Q4 estimate, the net cash bridge (tax owed and the bonus owed at 30 Sep, investment assets)
    and the preference discount are own assumptions; the sensitivities show their weight.
  - Samsung files with DART, not EDGAR; the share count and the Q3 balance sheet come from Yahoo and
    the IR deck, with no filing check this time. The GDR prices predate the report.
