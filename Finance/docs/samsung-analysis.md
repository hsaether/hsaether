## Summary

**The memory cycle** (read from `docs/ai-market-bbb.md`, H2 Memory cycle; no memory forecast is made here)
- **Where:** late upcycle. Contract prices still rise (Q4-26 conventional DRAM +10–15%, Q1-27 +3–8%), but q/q growth has halved twice since Q2-26; mobile DRAM and most NAND are about flat; margins are records (Samsung DS 72% in Q3); the shares peaked in June (Samsung −46% from 362,500 on 18 Jun).
- **What turns it:** new supply from 2H-27 into 2028 (Samsung's 2026 capex +60% to ~84tn and higher in 2027, P5 in 2028; SK hynix Yongin; Micron 1δ / G11; CXMT +31% in wafers) meeting AI capex digestion after the contracted 2027 build (K1 Base 1,120 → 1,170 → 1,170bn). LTAs soften the fall for the leaders and cap the rise.
- **How likely, when (AI BBB judgement): Base kept** — the turn in 2028; timing risk 1–2 quarters earlier for NAND and commodity DRAM; 2028 depth Base or shallower for the leaders; 2029–30 Base or deeper, the main risk.
- **Samsung's Q3 report (DART, 16 Nov) changes none of it:** no customer price, range, floor level or order value; customer prepayments of ~KRW 10tn arrived in H1 (contract liabilities 14.0 → 23.6tn at 30 Jun) and only +0.6tn in Q3 (24.1tn). The AI BBB memory-LTA signpost stays Bull-side, not crossed.
- **What the price implies:** at KRW 196,800 this build earns k if 2028–30 memory earnings come in at **0.45× the Base path** (.27 / .18 / .16 of CY27 consensus) and 0% at 0.23×. A 2019-depth fall (.45 / .30 / .25, ~0.74× Base) still returns ~24%. **The price discounts a cut from Base about twice that of a 2019-depth fall**, not a later or shallower turn. The AI BBB layer method gave 0.68× at 188,700 (6 Nov); this build is lower because net cash is 17% of the price at year-end and the cash return is fast.
