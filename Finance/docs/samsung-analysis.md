## Summary

**The memory cycle** (read from `docs/ai-market-bbb.md`, H2 Memory cycle and its 18 Nov turn call; no memory forecast is made here)
- **Where:** late upcycle. Contract prices still rise (Q4-26 conventional DRAM +10–15%, Q1-27 +3–8%), but momentum has slowed (PC DRAM "slower than last quarter", DDR5 spot soft); margins are records (Samsung DS 72% in Q3); the shares peaked in June (−53% from 374,500 at the 23 Nov close).
- **What turns it:** new supply from 2H-27 into 2028 (Samsung's 2026 capex +60% to ~84tn and higher in 2027, P5 in 2028; SK hynix Yongin; Micron 1δ / G11; CXMT) meeting AI capex digestion after the contracted 2027 build (K1 Base 1,120 → 1,170 → 1,170bn). LTAs soften the fall for the leaders and cap the rise.
- **The AI BBB's call (18 Nov, 60%, moved Base):** 2027 kept at **0.90**; **2028–30 deeper: .50 / .32 / .28** of CY27 consensus (was .60 / .40 / .35); revenue index 138 / 108 / 95. Families: slowing contract-price momentum (L2), the supplier capex step-up — Samsung's own included (L4), memory shares −33% to −41% (L5); against: HBM4 sold out, LTAs (L1). Reversal signpost: Q1-27 DRAM contract ≥ +10% q/q with spot above contract (early Jan).
- **What the price implies:** at KRW 177,500 this build earns k if 2028–30 memory earnings come in at **0.44× the new Base** (.22 / .14 / .12 of CY27 consensus) and 0% at 0.21×. A 2019-depth fall (.45 / .30 / .25) still returns ~30%. **The price discounts a fall about twice as deep as the AI BBB's already-deeper Base.** The AI BBB layer method gave 0.87× (18 Nov, at the 18 Nov price); this build is lower because net cash is 19% of the price at year-end and the cash return is fast.

**Verdict**

The named reason the market may be wrong: ~10tn of customer prepayments, 2027 HBM sold out at target prices and net cash of 19% of the price carry Samsung through a trough the price treats as permanent; the DX side gains when memory falls.
