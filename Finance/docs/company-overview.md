**How to read it**
- **Price at analysis / P_k / Base IRR / verdict at analysis** are the document's own figures (k = 12%; the price that returns 12% a year to end-2029 is **P_k**).
- **Margin at close** = P_k ÷ latest close − 1, **indicative**: it ignores time since the analysis (P_k drifts up ~1% a month), dividends and FX moves. Bands: clears above +2%, marginal ±2%, fails below −2%. For analyses older than two weeks (marked `old`) the column is rough.
