## Summary

**In plain terms**
1. **Where the return comes from:** cash from two businesses. SED's tender rigs pay out most of what they earn, and from January 2027 Ventura's two Brazilian rigs start Petrobras contracts of 2½ and 4 years at nearly double their old rates. Ventura is bought with new SED shares at a price well below what those contracts and rigs are worth on our numbers, so each SED share gets more cash than SED alone would give it.
2. **What must be true:** the deal closes on the letter-of-intent terms, both Brazilian rigs start on time, and Ventura keeps running its rigs at about $115k a day, as it does today.
3. **What breaks it:** Ventura's costs 2% higher, or Petrobras ending SSV Victoria's contract after 2½ years, already takes the combined case below 12%; Gulf jackups flooding Asia sinks the tender rigs as well (Bear −31%). If the deal fails, SED alone returns ~9% a year: it fails.

**Verdict: clears narrowly (combined Base).**
- **Base (deal closes):** IRR_T **13.0%** against k = 12%; P_k **NOK 6.73** against **NOK 6.58**: **margin of safety +2.2%**, just above the +2% band. Clears at or below NOK 6.60, fails above NOK 6.87.
- **Deal fails (standalone, v3.0 build):** 8.6%, P_k 6.10, −7.3%: fails. The combination adds ~4.4 pp of Base IRR.
- **Bear:** −30.7%, ~56% of the capital lost; the balance sheet survives (Ventura net debt peaks ~$210m at end-2026, covered by contracted Petrobras cash).
- **Bull:** 38.0%, P_k 11.11.
- **What the price needs:** Ventura exit marks ≥ 0.93× our Base and Ventura opex ≤ 1.02× today. **The result is thin and rests on Ventura's cost base and Petrobras keeping both rigs.**

**Base in brief**
- **Ventura leg:** DS Carolina 910 days at ~$370k and SSV Victoria 1,455 days at ~$320k for Petrobras from 15 Jan 2027 `[F/B]`; SSV Catarina wins the Eni Indonesia tender (6 + 6 months at ~$235k) `[A]`. EBITDA ~$163m in 2027–28 and $177m in 2029; net debt $210m → $61m by exit. Exit rigs on Rig BBB rates adjusted for spec ($310k drillship, $270k semi, Catarina $235k), at Ventura's own opex with normalised survey capex.
- **SED leg:** the v3.0 build unchanged (Rig BBB tender row 78 / 82 / 85, T-16 at $90k, EDrill-1 / T-15 re-let at $78k).
- **Deal terms:** 605m new SED shares (SED holders 55.0% on 738.6m diluted shares); closing 1 Mar 2027 `[A]`; the deferred H2-26 SED distribution ($40m, April 2027) goes to all combined holders `[A]` (SED holders only: +1.5 pp).

**Bear in brief**
- **Story:** the tender Bear (Gulf jackups into Asia, barges at $65–68k) plus a Ventura Bear: Petrobras ends Victoria at day 910 (Jul 2029), Carolina's option lapses, Catarina idle ~9 months then $205k, exit marks at Bear mid-cycle (drillship $275k, semis $230k).
- **Worst year:** 2029 (Ventura FCF negative, SED special surveys). Capital: ΣD + V_T = NOK 2.92 against 6.58 (−56%).
- **What does the damage:** the exit marks of the old rigs (Victoria and Catarina fall to near the $10m floor).
