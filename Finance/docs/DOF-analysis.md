### The DOFCON PLSV re-contract basis (Skandi Buzios, Recife and Olinda)

- **gross revenue**, before the JV's opex of ~$35–40k a day per vessel at 100% (2025: $76m ÷ 6 ÷ 365 = $34.7k; Q3-26: $39.9k);
- **at 100%** of the vessel (DOF reports 50%);

TechnipFMC's pipelay services are outside DOFCON; Norskan's marine operations revenue sits in DOF's Norskan segment;
- **per working day** (after downtime), and a six-vessel average across old, extended and 2024-round contracts;
- reported in USD; the BRL share of revenue and cost is `[?]`.

**What the round prices.** $305k (Buzios) and $295k (Recife, Olinda) per vessel per day at 100%, 4 years from January 2028 (Fearnley via Infront TDN `[E]`; DOF confirms the qualification and the start `[F]`).
