## Summary
**In plain terms**
1. **Where the return comes from:** the dividend, about 11–12% of the price a year. The fleet loses value as it ages, and the price now sits only ~8% below what we think the fleet is worth at normal rates, so that gap no longer covers the ageing.
2. **What must be true:** vessel values hold near normal-cycle levels to 2029 and DOF keeps paying out ~90% of its free cash.
3. **What breaks it:** at NOK 133.90 Base already returns ~10%, below 12%: it needs vessel values ~7.5% above our normal-cycle marks, or a takeover bid. A weak oil and North Sea market (Bear) loses about a quarter of the money.

**Verdict**

A bid premium is Bull-side optionality: a bid at NAV_0 (NOK 145.2) would be +8% on the price; the offer-price floor (~NOK 129) is below it.
