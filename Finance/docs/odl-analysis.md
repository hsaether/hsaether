## Summary

**Cycle first** (from Rig BBB, HE semis NCS row; no own rig forecast):
- **Where the cycle is: tight and firming.** NCS HE cover ~96% for 2027 and ~83% for 2028;
  leading edge $430–517k; Westwood Norway $436k for 2026 YTD (+$50k y/y). Odfjell calls most
  Tier-1 HE rigs booked for 2027 and many into 2028, with newbuilds unlikely. Transocean's NCS
  prints for 2027–29 starts: single rigs $441–519k, Equinor's multi-year package $402k (= Base
  mid-cycle).
- **What can turn it:** demand, not supply (consent-gated, no newbuilds). The Bear catalyst is oil
  Bear (Iran–USA deal plus an OPEC+ market-share fight; Brent 65 / 60 / 60) through FID deferrals
  and minimum NCS programmes; the hit lands in 2028–29 (MCU 88% → 83%, leading edge $400k → $380k).
  **When:** a stress test, not a probability; earliest Q1 2027 (FIDs); ODL's 2027 is fully booked.
- **What the price implies:** a through-life NCS HE rate of **~$408k** on Odfjell's own economics,
  against Base mid-cycle $400k; a 12% return needs **~$418k** (~5% above Base). The price assumes
  today's tightness lasts beyond 2029. The Bollsta purchase, the only HE transaction, implies ~$332k.

**Verdict: fails.**
- **Base IRR ~9%** against k = 12%. The hurdle price is **$9.97 (NOK 95.5)** against
  **$10.74 (NOK 102.80)**: a margin of safety of **−7%**.
- **Bear** (oil Bear; NCS MCU 83% by 2029; mid-cycle $350k): IRR ~−12%. Capital is not preserved
  ($7.79 against $10.74, a ~27% permanent loss); the balance sheet holds (net debt 1.1x EBITDA at
  exit, no maturity before Mar 2031).
- **Bull** (mid-cycle $460k; NCS leading edge $540k by 2029): IRR ~23%, hurdle $13.92 (NOK 133.3).
  The price reflects **19%** of the Base→Bull hurdle gap.
- **Price triggers (Oslo, NOK, ex dividend, at USD/NOK 9.5743):** Base = k at **NOK 95.5**; Bear
  preserves capital at **≤ NOK ~75**; Bull's hurdle NOK 133.3. The hurdle is in USD: 1% on USD/NOK
  moves the NOK hurdle by ~NOK 1.
- **Why it still fails at Base:** the cash yield is high (d 9.5%; $0.27 a quarter), but the fleet
  ages (−$283m) and the above-market firm contracts roll off (−$359m). The price sits 1.05x Base
  NAV_0; per-share value falls 1.0% a year while cash is paid out.
- **Robustness:** the verdict holds on every exit basis: Rig BBB's convention marks give Base IRR
  ~−11%, exit marks re-discounted at Oslo's mid-cycle return 7.0%. The decision basis (Odfjell's
  realised margins) is the generous one.
