## Conclusion

  returns and the market reference E / R and kept the NIBOR path, GDP and default rates).
- **Base:** Nordic HY earns about **7.2% a year in NOK (2.75 pp over NIBOR) through 2029** (8.0 /
  7.2 / 6.8% in 2027–29). The return falls as NIBOR follows Norges Bank's path down (4.8% → 3.9%),
  while spreads hold near the 5.0% normal. First-time defaults rise from the 2025 low (2.6%) to
  **3.0–3.5%**: energy issuers stay healthy on the upstream baselines; the risk sits in
  sponsor-backed continental and consumer issuers and Swedish finance and real estate.
- **Rates:** Norges Bank held 4.50% on 5 Nov (a hawkish hold) with no material change in the
  outlook, so **Base keeps the MPR 3/26 path** to MPR 4/26 (17 Dec). The market path sits +0.17 /
  +0.33 / +0.69 pp above Base (10 Nov): the risk is upward for 2028–29. October CPI-ATE 2.7% is on
  Norges Bank's projection. The Riksbank hiked to 2.00%; its minutes point to about two more hikes.
- **Spreads:** the Nordic anchor widened 60 bp to **490 bp** in October (Europe +90, US +50 bp)
  `[E]`; the widening is broad, not only the Nordic distressed names (Intrum, Trustly, HX, Borr,
  Archer), and Heimdal Høyrente had ~9% net redemptions. DNB Carnegie Nordic HY returned −0.2% in
  October (YTD +5.6%) `[E]`. Early November eased globally; the November print is a checkpoint.
- **Defaults:** pace over 1 Sep–5 Nov ~2.2% a year `[A]`, inside Base's 2.0–4.0%; held names Borr
  Watch, HX super senior Stressed, Intrum and Trustly Distressed (`docs/hy-issuers.md`).
- **Row ranking by risk premium (spread − expected loss, Base), strongest → weakest:** (1) **Oil
  Service** ~440 bp (rig and OSV baselines firm through 2029); (2) **O&G E&P** ~430 bp, but 8.3% FTD
  in 2025 (small single-asset issuers); (3) **Other non-energy** ~345 bp, where most 2026 defaults
  are; (4) **Real Estate** ~300 bp (Swedish FTD 3.0% in 2025, but the Riksbank is hiking). Shipping:
  no sector spread `[?]`. Premiums use the 2025 new-issue spreads (NT), not re-marked for October.
- **Main Bear catalyst:** oil Bear (a durable Iran–USA deal plus an OPEC+ market-share fight) with
  a global recession in H1 2027, hitting the 25% energy block and leveraged non-energy issuers at
  once: spreads ~850 bp by end-2027, defaults peak ~8.6% in 2028 (the 2016 analogue), return −0.4%
  in 2027, recovered through carry in 2028–29. The deal leg is closer after the midterms.
- **Main Bull catalyst:** a soft landing: inflation falls faster, Norges Bank cuts from mid-2027,
  oil on its Base path, Nordic spreads towards ~390–400 bp (10.7% in 2027).
