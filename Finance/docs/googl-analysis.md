- **Q3: revenue on the model, earnings behind it.** Revenue $127.6bn (+25%) as modelled, but the mix
  turned: **Google Cloud +103% to $30.7bn** (model $28.8bn) with TPU system sales "a meaningful
  contributor", **Google Services +11% to $96.3bn** (model $98.4bn; Search +12% after +17% in Q2).
  Operating margin **31.8%** (Q2 34.0%). GAAP EPS $2.12 carries a **−$0.55 loss on equity
  securities** (about the SpaceX mark); **core EPS $2.67 against ~$2.94–3.01 consensus** and $3.00 in

  FCF −$9.1bn; no buybacks; **$12.4bn of new shares sold through the ATM**, more than twice the
  employee withholding.
- **The model fell, the price fell more.** Base core EPS 2027 $13.45 → **$12.67** (−6%: Services

  ~0.25× EBITDA).
- **What the price needs:** an exit P/E of **~22.8** on 2030 EPS (Base 21; 5-year median forward
  P/E 20.8), or EPS growth of **~14.4% a year 2028–30** (Base 11.3%). Single operating levers come
  close without getting there: Cloud +5 pp a year gives 11.4%, Services +2 pp a year 11.4%, Waymo

  55% within 24 months); TPU revenue ramps in 2027. Nothing the model's rows read moved; only the
