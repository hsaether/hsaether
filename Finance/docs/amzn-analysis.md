## Verdict and caveat

  **$270.87 against P_k $222.2** (USD, Nasdaq; re-mark 11 Nov at the 10 Nov close). Base IRR 5.2%

  break hits AWS's largest AI customers (also two equity holdings) in a recession that also cuts
  retail margins, and the P/E falls to the E-layer Bear.
- **What would flip it:** an exit P/E of ≥ ~27.0 (Amazon's own 5-year low is ~22.75), or a price at
  or below **~$222.2** (where Base meets k). At $270.87 none of the single operating sensitivities

  its margin near 37% 8.5%, AWS on the E2 Bull row 9.3%. The Anthropic range cannot flip it: even
  the top of the range adds 0.3 pp.
- **Caveat:** a margin-of-safety screen, not an intrinsic valuation or a price target. Returns are
  before fund fees and tax; NOK returns are a memo on the Equity BBB USD/NOK path.
